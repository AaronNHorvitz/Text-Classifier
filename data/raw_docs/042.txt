Dear BYDS 3's, Pre-K and K Families,
Beginning next Wednesday, October 27th, we will launch our Occupational Therapy screening 
program. Karen Fisher and Jennifer Matsu, both Occupational Therapists and founders of 
Children’s TheraFun, will provide this service at school.
 
The OT screening evaluates if your child for age-appropriate skills in gross motor, fine motor, 
visual motor, visual perceptual and sensory processing skills.
We understand that having your child screened for Occupational Therapy may not be a priority 
for you at this time. If you would like to have your child participate in the screening, 
please complete this form no later than Friday, October 22nd.  If this form is not 
returned, we will assume you do not want your child to participate and will not be scheduled.
After the screening is completed, each parent will receive the results. If a problem is noted, 
recommendations will be made for further testing. It is important to remember that this initial 
procedure is a screening tool and not an in-depth evaluation. If you have any questions or 
information you would like to share with either Ms. Matsu or Ms. Fisher, please send a note 
prior to the screening to Jenny (for all 3’s) at JMark@byds.org or Ms. Hebert (for Prek and K) at 
CHebert@byds.org.
With health and safety as our top priority, Ms. Matsu and Ms. Fisher will follow all current BYDS 
COVID-19 protocols.
