Dear Barry, John, Ed, and Yo-Yo,

I am writing to formally apologize for the unforeseen system glitch that occurred during our testing phase last week. As Chief Engineer of Cardiff Electric's Engineering department, I take full responsibility for the oversight and assure you that we are taking immediate action to rectify the issue.

A thorough analysis revealed a minor bug in the code, which was inadvertently overlooked during the QA process. Our team has already begun implementing corrective measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.

Please be assured that our commitment to quality remains unwavering, and I am confident that we will emerge stronger from this experience.
