Dear Yo-Yo, Cameron, and Barry,

I'm writing to express my frustration regarding the recent system outage. The root cause analysis indicates that the issue was due to an unhandled exception in the database query, specifically a missing 'AND' statement in the WHERE clause (see snippet below).

```
SELECT * FROM orders
WHERE order_date > '1983-01-01'
```

This oversight has resulted in significant downtime and loss of productivity. I've reviewed the logs and identified several instances where this issue could have been caught during testing, had we employed a more rigorous approach to QA.

I understand that we're all working under tight deadlines, but it's imperative that we prioritize quality control to avoid such costly mistakes in the future.
