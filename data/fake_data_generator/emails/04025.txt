Dear Joe and Cameron,

I've been following the latest developments in modem technology, and I'm excited to share some observations with you. The recent release of the Hayes Smartmodem 2400 has sparked significant interest in the industry. Its ability to achieve data transfer rates of up to 9600 baud is a notable improvement over previous models.

Furthermore, I've been analyzing the patent applications filed by various companies, including CompuServe and Microsoft. Their approaches to improving modem efficiency and error correction are particularly noteworthy.

I believe these advancements will have a significant impact on our work at Cardiff Electric. As we continue to develop our own modem technology, it's essential that we stay informed about the latest innovations in the field.
