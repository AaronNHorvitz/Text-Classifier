Hello Cameron, Joe, Yo-Yo, and Malcolm,

As we continue to strive for excellence in our work environment, I wanted to bring to your attention some important health and safety considerations. As you know, our team has been working diligently on several projects, and it's essential that we prioritize our well-being while doing so.

In particular, I'd like to emphasize the importance of ergonomic best practices when working at our desks. Proper posture, adequate lighting, and regular breaks can significantly reduce the risk of eye strain, back discomfort, and other common issues. To support this, I've included some simple guidelines below:

* Adjust your chair height to keep your feet flat on the floor or on a footrest.
* Position your monitor directly in front of you, at a comfortable distance, with the top edge at eye level or slightly below.
* Ensure your keyboard is placed directly in front of your body, with your wrists straight and elbows at a 90-degree angle.

By following these simple tips, we can minimize the risk of discomfort and fatigue. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to me.
