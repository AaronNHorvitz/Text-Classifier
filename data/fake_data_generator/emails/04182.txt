Hi Ed, Joe, Donna, Larry, Gordon, and Yo-Yo,

Compliance is like trying to tame a wild mustang - you can try to corral it, but it'll always find a way to break free. I'm not saying that's a bad thing, per se. I mean, who doesn't love a little rebellion?

But seriously, I wanted to touch base with everyone about our progress on the compliance front. We're making slow but steady progress, and I think we're finally starting to get a handle on what these regulations are actually asking us to do.

Of course, that's not to say it's been easy. Lord knows I've spent more hours than I care to admit staring at spreadsheets and wondering if I'm just plain crazy for trying to make sense of this stuff.

So, here's the plan: let's get together next week and go over our findings so far. Maybe we can even come up with some creative solutions that won't drive us all completely bonkers.

Take care, and don't forget to bring your A-game (and a healthy dose of skepticism)!
