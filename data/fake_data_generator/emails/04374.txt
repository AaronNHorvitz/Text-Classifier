Hi John, Donna, Yo-Yo, Debbie,

I'm writing to you with a heavy heart because Cardiff Electric has just informed me that they're cutting funding for our department. I know, I know - it's like someone poured kerosene on the dream and lit it on fire.

We've all worked so hard to get this far, but it seems that the suits have decided we're not worth the investment anymore. I'm still trying to wrap my head around it, but I guess that's what happens when you try to innovate in a company that values stability above all else.

I know some of you might be thinking, "What does this mean for our project?" Well, let me tell you - it means we're stuck in neutral while the rest of the world zooms past us. But I'm not one to give up easily. I think we can still make something of this mess, and I'd love to get your thoughts on how we can turn things around.

Let's grab lunch and talk about it?
