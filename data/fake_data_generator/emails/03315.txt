Future of Event Planning at Cardiff Electric


Dear Cameron,

I'm writing to you today because I need someone to share my frustration. We're stuck in neutral, and I don't know how much longer I can keep trying to push this company forward. Our event planning process is a relic from the past - cumbersome, inefficient, and ineffective. It's like we're trying to make a soufflé with a sledgehammer.

We need to rethink everything. We need to be bold, take risks, and innovate. I want us to challenge every assumption, every process, and every person involved in event planning. I want us to dream big, not just think small.
