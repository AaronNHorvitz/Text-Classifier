Subject:    Health and Safety Protocols for New Hardware

Dear Cameron, Donna, Ed,

I'm writing to propose a set of protocols for ensuring the safe integration of our new hardware into the manufacturing process. As you know, I've been working tirelessly to optimize the design and functionality of our latest prototype, and I believe it's essential we take a proactive approach to minimizing risks.

The new hardware, as you're aware, incorporates advanced microprocessor technology, which presents unique challenges in terms of electromagnetic interference (EMI) and radio-frequency interference (RFI). To mitigate these risks, I recommend implementing a series of checks and balances during the installation process. Specifically, I suggest we:

1. Conduct thorough EMI/RFI testing on all new hardware components before integration into the production line.
2. Establish clear procedures for handling and storing sensitive equipment to prevent damage or contamination.
3. Provide comprehensive training to all engineers and technicians on the proper installation and maintenance of the new hardware.

By taking these precautions, I'm confident we can reduce the risk of errors, malfunctions, or even catastrophic failures. As Chief Engineer, it's my responsibility to ensure our products are not only innovative but also safe for our employees and customers.

I'd appreciate any feedback you may have on this proposal. Let me know if you're available to discuss further.
