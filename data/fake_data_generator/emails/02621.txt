Dear Ed, Barry, Larry and Cameron,

I'm writing to bring up a matter that's been weighing on my mind. The recent batch of 80486 CPUs has shown some...unpredictable behavior. I've run some tests, and it seems we're looking at a possible defect in the manufacturing process. I think it would be prudent to recall the affected units and re-test them.

I know this might seem like a minor issue, but trust me when I say that a faulty CPU can cause all sorts of problems downstream. I've seen it happen before - it's a domino effect. We can't afford to have our reputation tarnished by a preventable mistake.
