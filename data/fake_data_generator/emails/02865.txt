Dear Larry, Malcolm, Yo-Yo, and John,

I am compelled to express my surprise, albeit tempered by professionalism, regarding the recent events that have transpired. In light of these unforeseen circumstances, I am writing to extend an apology for any inconvenience or distress caused.

Please be assured that Cardiff Electric's Legal department is actively engaged in addressing this matter and will continue to monitor developments closely. As Senior Counsel, it is my duty to ensure that all necessary measures are taken to protect the interests of our company.

I would appreciate it if you could bring me up to date on any further developments as soon as possible.
