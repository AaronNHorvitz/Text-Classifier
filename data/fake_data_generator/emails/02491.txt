Betrayal at Cardiff Electric


Dear Gordon, Ed, and Larry,

Listen up, folks. I'm writing to you with a heavy heart because I feel like I've been punched in the gut. I trusted everyone on this team, and I thought we were all working towards the same goal. But now, I'm not so sure.

Look, I get it. We're all trying to make a name for ourselves at Cardiff Electric. And yeah, sometimes that means making tough decisions or compromising our values. But when it comes down to it, I thought we had each other's backs. Newsflash: we don't. Not anymore, anyway.
