As we continue to refine our manufacturing process, I've been giving a great deal of thought to the quality assurance measures we need to put in place. Specifically, I believe it's essential that we establish a more robust testing regimen for our newly designed 286 boards.

I'd love to discuss these ideas further with the team and explore ways we can implement them within our existing resource constraints.
