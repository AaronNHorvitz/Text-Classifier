Dear everyone,

Listen up, because I'm only going to say this once. We're stuck in a rut, and I'm not just talking about our code. I mean, have you seen the training materials we've been using? It's like they were written by someone who has never actually used a computer.

I know some of you might be thinking, "Cameron, what's your problem?" And my problem is that we're not innovating enough. We're not pushing the boundaries. We're not catching fire! (Sorry, had to.)

So here's the deal. I want to propose an idea for a new training program. It'll be hands-on, project-based, and actually relevant to what we do. And it won't just be about checking boxes on a list of "skills learned." It'll be about creating something that matters.

I know some of you might be thinking, "But Cameron, this is Cardiff Electric. We're not exactly known for our cutting-edge training programs." And you're right. But I say, why can't we be? Why can't we be the ones who actually innovate in this field?

So what do you say? Are you with me on this?
