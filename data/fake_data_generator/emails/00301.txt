Congratulations are in order! We've successfully resolved the issue with the 286 motherboard. It was a team effort, but our technical expertise and troubleshooting skills truly shone through.

1. Power off the machine.
2. Locate the capacitor in question (it's usually marked with a 'C' and a value).
3. Replace it with an identical or equivalent one.
