Subject:    Internal Announcement: Compliance Guidelines for New Projects

Dear Gordon, Debbie, Malcolm, and Larry,

As we continue to move forward with new projects at Cardiff Electric, it is essential that we maintain a high level of compliance with our internal guidelines and regulatory requirements. To ensure a smooth transition, I am issuing this internal announcement to clarify the process for new project submissions.

All new project proposals must be submitted through the designated online portal, which will trigger a comprehensive review by the Legal Department. This review will encompass a thorough examination of the proposed project's compliance with company policies, regulatory frameworks, and industry standards.

Please note that any deviations from established guidelines or unapproved modifications to existing projects may result in delays or even termination of the project. It is imperative that all team members adhere to these guidelines to avoid potential legal and reputational risks.

I encourage you to reach out to me or our Legal Department with any questions or concerns regarding this announcement. We are committed to supporting your efforts and ensuring a successful outcome for each new project.
