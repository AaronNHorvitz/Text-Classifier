Dear Team,

I am writing to outline our strategy for training and development within the Project Management department. As we move forward with the Turbo project, it's essential that we prioritize upskilling and reskilling our team members to meet the demands of this ambitious initiative.

Here are the key objectives:

• Identify knowledge gaps and develop a plan to fill them
• Develop a comprehensive training program for Turbo-related skills
• Establish a mentorship program to facilitate knowledge sharing
• Set clear performance metrics and goals for each team member

I expect all team members to actively participate in this process and provide feedback on their own training needs. I will be conducting regular check-ins to monitor progress and address any concerns.

Let's work together to make Cardiff Electric a leader in innovation and excellence!
