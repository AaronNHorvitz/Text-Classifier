I am compelled to share a milestone in my personal life, which I believe warrants notification. As of today, I have completed 500 hours of reading "The Oxford English Dictionary" in its entirety. This achievement has not only refined my understanding of the English language but also instilled a sense of accomplishment.

In a world where precision is paramount, it is a testament to the human spirit's capacity for diligence and perseverance.

EMAIL BODY

Please join me in celebrating this modest victory.
