Dear Gordon,

I wanted to follow up on our discussion regarding error handling in the BIOS firmware. After further review, I realized that my previous approach was not entirely correct.

In fact, I've been experimenting with a different approach using the bitwise OR operator (`|`) to combine error flags. Here's an example of how this could be implemented:
```c
if ((bios_error & 0x01) | (disk_error & 0x02)) {
    // handle errors
}
```
I believe this method would be more efficient and robust than the original solution.

Please let me know your thoughts on this revised approach.
