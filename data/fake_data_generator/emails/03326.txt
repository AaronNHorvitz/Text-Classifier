Subject:    Bitter Truths from the Industry Front Lines


Hi John, Larry, Gordon, and Malcolm,

I'm writing to share some bitter truths about the industry that's been stewing in my brain. It seems like every time we make progress, someone else swoops in and steals our thunder (or at least tries to). I mean, have you seen those new IBM PCs? They're basically just souped-up versions of what we've been working on here at Cardiff Electric!

And don't even get me started on the whole "PC clones" thing. It's like everyone else is trying to ride our coattails and claim they invented the wheel (or in this case, the personal computer). Newsflash: we were doing it first, folks!

I know I sound like a grumpy cat sometimes, but someone's gotta keep us all on our toes. Just remember, when the going gets tough, the tough get coding... or something like that.
