I am writing to outline a proposed compliance framework for our software development projects at Cardiff Electric. As we continue to grow and expand, it is essential that we maintain a structured approach to ensure regulatory compliance. I have attached the draft framework for your review.

The proposed framework consists of three primary components: 1) risk assessment, 2) controls implementation, and 3) ongoing monitoring. I will provide more details on each component below:

Risk Assessment:
We will conduct regular risk assessments to identify potential compliance risks and prioritize mitigation efforts accordingly.

Controls Implementation:
We will implement a suite of controls to mitigate identified risks, including documentation, testing, and auditing procedures.

Ongoing Monitoring:
We will maintain an ongoing monitoring process to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and identify areas for improvement.
