Dear All,

I'm writing to remind everyone about some critical health and safety protocols we need to follow in our department. As you know, I've been dealing with a lot of frustration lately trying to get people to take these things seriously. It's not just about following rules; it's about being responsible for your own well-being and the well-being of those around you.

Firstly, please make sure you're wearing your safety glasses when working on any equipment or machinery that requires them. I've seen far too many close calls because people thought they were "just quickly" doing something without proper protection.

Secondly, if you notice any hazards or defects in our workspace, don't hesitate to report them to me or Malcolm. We need everyone's help to keep this place safe and clean.

Lastly, please be mindful of your own physical limits when lifting heavy objects or performing repetitive tasks. I've had to deal with more than a few injuries that could have been prevented if people just took a minute to think about what they're doing.

That's it for now. Let's all do our part to keep each other safe and healthy.
