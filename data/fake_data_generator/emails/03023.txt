As you know, I've always been meticulous about ensuring our products meet certain standards. However, in this day and age, even the smallest mistakes can have far-reaching consequences. I'm concerned that if we don't take proactive measures to address any potential issues, it could damage our reputation irreparably.

I'd like to schedule a meeting with you to discuss this further and explore possible solutions. Perhaps we can revisit some of the older designs and see how they might be modified to prevent similar problems in the future.
