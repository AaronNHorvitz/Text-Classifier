New Developments in PC Architecture


Dear Larry,

I'm writing to share some thoughts on recent advancements in personal computer design. As you know, Cardiff Electric has been exploring ways to optimize our systems for maximum efficiency. In my opinion, the latest breakthroughs in microprocessor technology hold significant promise.

The new Intel 80286 processor, in particular, offers improved memory management and enhanced I/O capabilities. I've been experimenting with prototype designs incorporating this chip, and the results are promising. However, I'm grappling with some unforeseen issues related to bus architecture and addressing modes. I'd appreciate your insights on this matter.
