Hi Debbie, Joe, John,

I'm writing to follow up on our previous discussions regarding health and safety protocols at Cardiff Electric. As you know, we've been pushing the boundaries of what's possible with computer technology, but that doesn't mean we can ignore the risks involved. I'm not saying we're playing with fire, but... well, actually, yes, we are.

In all seriousness, it's crucial we establish clear guidelines for handling hazardous materials and equipment. I've included a draft policy below for your review and feedback.

**Health and Safety Policy Draft**

* Proper disposal of chemicals and electronic waste
* Use of personal protective equipment (PPE) when working with hazardous materials
* Regular inspections of workstations and equipment

I'd love to hear your thoughts on this preliminary draft. Let's make sure we're all on the same page before implementing any new procedures.
