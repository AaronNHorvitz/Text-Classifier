Dear John, Gordon, Barry, Larry, and Donna,

I'm excited to share my findings with you regarding the Wellness Check initiative. As we discussed during our meeting last week, I've been reviewing the system logs and have identified a few areas where we can improve our monitoring tools.

One key finding is that the CPU utilization on our servers has been trending higher than expected, particularly during peak usage hours. I've written a quick script to help us track this trend more effectively: `ps -eo pid,%cpu | awk '{print $1,$2}'`. This will give us a better understanding of where our resources are being consumed.

Another area for improvement is our error handling mechanism. By implementing a try-catch block in our code, we can significantly reduce the number of unhandled exceptions and improve overall system stability. Here's an example: `try { ... } catch (e) { log.error("Error occurred: " + e.getMessage()); }`.

I'd love to discuss these findings further and explore ways to implement changes. Please let me know if you're available for a meeting this week.
