Dear Debbie,

I'm writing to provide a detailed analysis of the system outage that occurred last night. As Chief Engineer, I've taken the liberty of reviewing the logs and conducting some preliminary tests. Preliminary findings suggest that the issue was caused by a misaligned disk controller, which triggered a cascade failure.

In my opinion, this is an isolated incident that can be easily resolved with a simple firmware update. However, I do recommend we schedule a thorough review of our system redundancy protocols to prevent similar incidents in the future.
