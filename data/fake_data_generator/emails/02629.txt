
From:    Donna Clark

Subject: Condolences on Joe McMillan's Passing


Dear Malcolm, Barry, Debbie,

I am writing to express my sincerest condolences on the passing of Joe McMillan. As you know, he was a key player in our efforts to revitalize Cardiff Electric's fortunes, and his absence will be deeply felt.

While I understand that this may not have been a formal partnership, Joe's contributions to our team were invaluable. His passion and expertise will not soon be forgotten.
