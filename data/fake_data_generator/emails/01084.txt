Dear Team,

I'm delighted to share that our category algorithm update is nearing completion. As a quick recap, we're implementing a modified version of the Jaccard similarity coefficient (JS) to improve our product categorization. The updated formula will be as follows:

`category_similarity = 1 - |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B|`

where A and B are the two categories being compared.

I'll be testing this update on a sample dataset later today. If you have any questions or would like to review the changes, please don't hesitate to reach out. I'm confident that this enhancement will lead to more accurate product recommendations for our users.
