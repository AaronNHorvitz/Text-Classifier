Hi Yo-Yo, Joe, Gordon, and Donna,

I'm following up on our previous discussions regarding compliance with industry standards. Specifically, we've encountered an issue with our latest batch of IBM-compatible PCs running PC-DOS 1.1. Upon further investigation, it appears that the system is not compatible with certain DOS applications due to a minor bug in the ROM BIOS.

To resolve this, I recommend updating the ROM BIOS to the latest version (available on our internal server) and re-testing the affected systems. Additionally, please ensure that any new PC-DOS installations are performed using the updated BIOS image.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this process, please don't hesitate to reach out. I'm more than happy to assist you in getting your systems up-to-date and compliant with industry standards.
