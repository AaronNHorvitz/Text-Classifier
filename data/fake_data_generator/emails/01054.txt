Subject:    Industry News: IBM's OS/2 Warp

Dear Team,

I've been following recent developments regarding IBM's OS/2 Warp, and I think it's essential we discuss its implications on our industry. Specifically, their efforts to integrate multimedia capabilities have piqued my interest.

In their presentation at the recent COMDEX show, they showcased some impressive demos of video and audio playback using the new multimedia APIs. I've included a snippet from their code sample below:

```cpp
#include <os2.h>
#include <mmr.h>

int main() {
    HMMR mmr = NULL;
    if (MmrOpen(&mmr, 0) == MMR_ERROR) {
        // Handle error
    }
    // Play some audio!
    MmrPlay(mmr, 0);
    return 0;
}
```

While I understand the potential benefits of OS/2 Warp's multimedia capabilities, I'm concerned about the potential impact on our existing software stack. I'd appreciate any thoughts or insights you may have on this matter.
