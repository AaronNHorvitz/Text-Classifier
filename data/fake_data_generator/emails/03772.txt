Subject: Training and Development Objectives for Q2

Dear Malcolm,

I am pleased to outline our training and development objectives for the second quarter of 1983. As we continue to drive innovation at Cardiff Electric, it is essential that our team members possess the skills and knowledge necessary to stay ahead of the curve.

Here are the key objectives:

• Conduct a comprehensive review of current skill sets among project management staff
• Develop a customized training program to address identified gaps
• Implement a mentorship program for junior project managers

I believe these initiatives will not only enhance our team's performance but also foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
