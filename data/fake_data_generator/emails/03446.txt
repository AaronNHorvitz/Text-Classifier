Subject: Product Recall


Dear John,

I'm writing to express my reservations regarding the recent product recall. As Chief Engineer, I have a responsibility to ensure that our products meet the highest standards of quality and safety. However, I'm concerned that the hasty nature of this recall may not be entirely justified.

Upon reviewing the incident reports, I've noticed some inconsistencies in the data. It seems that the faulty components were not uniformly distributed across all units. In fact, most of the affected products showed no signs of malfunction during routine testing. This raises questions about the scope and methodology of the recall.

I'd like to schedule a meeting with you to discuss this further and explore alternative solutions that might address the root cause of the issue without resorting to a blanket recall.
