I'm writing to follow up on our previous discussions regarding the Wellness Check category. As you know, we've been experiencing some frustrating issues with the system's self-diagnostic reports. After conducting a thorough analysis, I've identified the root cause of the problem: an incorrect configuration setting in the system's firmware.

To resolve this issue, please try the following troubleshooting steps:

1. Access the system's settings menu and ensure that the "Self-Diagnose" option is set to "Enabled."
2. Save the changes and restart the system.
3. Run a new Wellness Check report to verify that the self-diagnostic function is working correctly.

If you continue to experience issues after attempting these steps, please don't hesitate to reach out to me directly. I'm more than happy to assist further.
