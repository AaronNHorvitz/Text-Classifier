Subject: Training and Development Initiative

Dear Ed,

I'm writing to propose a new training initiative focused on advanced programming languages and software development methodologies. As we continue to push the boundaries of what's possible with our proprietary operating system, I believe it's essential that our engineering team stays ahead of the curve.

The current training schedule has not been yielding the desired results. Our engineers are struggling to keep up with the pace of innovation, and I'm concerned that this lack of proficiency will hinder our ability to deliver high-quality products. By incorporating cutting-edge programming languages like Pascal and C++, we can ensure that our team is equipped to tackle the most complex challenges.

I've outlined a tentative plan below:

* Initial training sessions to be conducted by external experts (estimated 2-3 weeks)
* Follow-up workshops to focus on practical applications and hands-on experience (estimated 4-6 weeks)
* Ongoing support and mentoring to ensure successful implementation of new skills

Please let me know if you're available to discuss this proposal further. I'd appreciate any feedback or suggestions you may have.
