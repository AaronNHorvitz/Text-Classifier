Dear Debbie, Cameron, Yo-Yo, and Joe,

As we continue to develop and refine our software solutions at Cardiff Electric, I'd like to propose a set of new standards for our Software Development department. In an effort to increase efficiency and reduce errors, I've identified several key areas that can benefit from standardized procedures.

Specifically, I recommend implementing the following:

* Use of consistent naming conventions for variables and functions (e.g., camelCase for JavaScript, underscore_separated for Python)
* Adoption of a unified coding style guide (e.g., PEP 8 for Python, Google Java Style Guide for Java)
* Mandatory code reviews for all commits to ensure adherence to these standards

By establishing these guidelines, I believe we can improve the overall quality and maintainability of our software. I'd appreciate any feedback or suggestions you may have on this proposal.
