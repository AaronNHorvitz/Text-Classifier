Dear Joe,

I'm writing to you with a heavy heart. Your enthusiasm is palpable, but I fear we're getting ahead of ourselves. A press release about "revolutionizing" the PC industry? It sounds like hubris to me.

We've had our share of failures and setbacks at Cardiff Electric. I'm not convinced that we're ready to make such bold claims. What's our unique value proposition again? Was it the user-friendly interface? The affordable prices? Unparalleled performance? If so, how do those features set us apart from the competition?

I'm just a Project Manager, Joe. I don't want to be the one responsible for blowing smoke up people's backsides.

Very truly yours,
