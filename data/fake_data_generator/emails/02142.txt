Dear Barry,

I've been giving some thought to our current health and safety protocols in the Engineering department. As you know, I'm always looking for ways to improve our processes and minimize risks. In light of recent events, I believe it's essential we revisit our emergency procedures and ensure everyone is aware of their roles.

In particular, I'd like to implement a revised protocol for handling chemical spills. Our current procedure is adequate, but I think we can do better by adding some additional steps to ensure containment and minimization of exposure.

I've outlined my proposed changes below:

1. Chemical spill response team: We'll establish a dedicated team to handle spills, ensuring prompt containment and proper disposal.
2. Personal protective equipment (PPE) usage: All personnel involved in chemical handling will be required to wear PPE at all times when working with hazardous materials.

I'd like your input on these proposals before we move forward with implementation. Please let me know if you have any questions or concerns.
