Hi John,

Look, I don't know how to sugarcoat this. I'm still trying to wrap my head around the way things went down with IBM. I mean, we were supposed to be partners, not adversaries. It feels like you got screwed over just as much as us.

I know you're trying to put the past behind us and move forward, but it's hard for me to shake off the feeling that we got played. You know me well enough to know that I'm a straight shooter, and I expect the same in return. When things get murky like this, it's tough for me to trust.

That being said, I do believe in Cardiff Electric, and I care about what happens here. So, if you're willing to have an honest conversation about what went wrong and how we can learn from it, I'm game. Let's talk turkey.
