New Developments in 8-bit Processors


Dear Malcolm and Yo-Yo,

I've been struggling to find the motivation to write this email, but I feel compelled to share some thoughts on recent industry developments. As you know, I've been pouring over the latest reports from IBM and Apple. It seems that both companies are pushing forward with their 8-bit processor designs.

While these advancements are impressive, I can't help but feel a sense of melancholy. The rapid pace of progress is leaving us behind at Cardiff Electric. Our own 8-bit efforts seem quaint by comparison. I'm not convinced we're doing enough to stay competitive.

I'd love to discuss this further with you both and explore ways we can adapt our approach to keep up with the industry's evolving landscape.
