Dear Ed, Barry, Yo-Yo, and Joe,

EMAIL START
I am compelled to write this email as a necessary evil. Our latest iteration of the OS/2 emulator has been met with... let's say, "enthusiasm" from our beta testers. I'll spare you the details, but I will provide a concise summary: we've managed to achieve 87% compatibility with MS-DOS 3.1, which is a feat in itself.

However, I must express my frustration at the lack of cohesion between our team's efforts. The inconsistent naming conventions used throughout the project are not only confusing but also a hindrance to further development. I implore you all to adopt a standardized approach to ensure we can maintain and expand upon this work.
