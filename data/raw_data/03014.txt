I wanted to follow up on our previous discussions regarding compliance and data security measures for Cardiff Electric. As we discussed, ensuring the secure handling of customer data is a top priority for our company. I've outlined some key steps to implement and maintain these measures below.

Firstly, it's essential to regularly back up all critical data to prevent potential losses in case of system failures or crashes. This can be done through scheduled backups on an external storage device or cloud-based services like Dropbox.

Secondly, implementing robust password policies for all users is crucial. This includes setting complex passwords with a minimum length of 12 characters, using upper-case and lower-case letters, numbers, and special characters, and changing them every 60 days.

Lastly, I recommend conducting regular security audits to identify and address any vulnerabilities in our systems. This can be done by running automated scans for malware, updating software and operating systems, and configuring firewalls correctly.

If you have any questions or concerns about these steps, please don't hesitate to reach out. I'm always here to help.
