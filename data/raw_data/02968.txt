Dear Ed, Debbie, Cameron, Yo-Yo, and Barry,

I've been pondering the recent discussions regarding research collaboration, and I must confess that my own contributions have been met with...silence. It's as if my ideas are being digested, only to be excreted without any tangible consequences. The notion of intellectual property ownership has taken on a life of its own in my mind, threatening to suffocate the very innovation we're trying to foster.

In light of this existential crisis, I propose we establish a more formalized process for tracking and sharing research findings. It's imperative that we create a culture where knowledge is not hoarded, but rather freely disseminated throughout the organization. This will enable us to build upon each other's discoveries, rather than duplicating efforts in isolation.

As we move forward, I suggest we establish a centralized repository for storing and categorizing our research endeavors. This would facilitate collaboration, reduce redundancy, and provide a means for monitoring progress.
