Quality Assurance Objectives for Q1 1983

Hi Debbie, Gordon, Larry, Yo-Yo, and Joe,

As we move into the new year, I want to emphasize the importance of our Quality Assurance efforts. Our objectives are clear: reduce defects by 15%, increase customer satisfaction ratings by 10%, and implement a robust testing framework for all projects. Here's how we're going to achieve these goals:

• Conduct a thorough review of current processes and procedures
• Identify and prioritize areas for improvement
• Develop and implement new quality control measures
• Establish regular reporting metrics for tracking progress

I've outlined the key tasks and deadlines below:

* Week 1-2: Review existing processes and procedures
* Week 3-4: Prioritize areas for improvement and develop new measures
* Week 5-6: Implement new quality control measures
* Ongoing: Track progress through regular reporting metrics

I'm confident that with our collective efforts, we can meet these objectives and demonstrate Cardiff Electric's commitment to excellence.
