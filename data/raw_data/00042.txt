Health and Safety Reminder: Ergonomic Workstations

Hi Ed, Gordon, Yo-Yo, Debbie, Cameron,

As we continue to push the boundaries of innovation at Cardiff Electric, it's essential we prioritize our well-being in the process. Our health and safety record has been exceptional thus far, but I'd like to reinforce some key reminders regarding ergonomic workstations.

In particular, please ensure that all computer monitors are positioned directly in front of your seated position, with the top edge at eye level or slightly below. This simple adjustment can significantly reduce the risk of long-term vision strain and discomfort.

Additionally, I recommend taking regular breaks to stretch and move around. We've found that a 10-minute break every hour can make a substantial difference in overall job satisfaction and productivity.

Remember, your well-being is our top priority at Cardiff Electric. If you have any questions or concerns regarding these guidelines, please don't hesitate to reach out to me directly.
