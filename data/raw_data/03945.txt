2. Ensure that the disk drive is properly seated in its bay.
3. Consider upgrading to a faster disk controller.

I've also found that the system's memory management could be improved by implementing some simple optimization techniques, such as:

1. Defragmenting the hard drive regularly.
2. Closing unnecessary applications when not in use.
3. Adjusting the swap file size and location.
