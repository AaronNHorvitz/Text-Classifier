Apology for Recent Error


Dear John,

I wanted to take a moment to address the recent issue with our software. Upon further investigation, I've determined that the error was caused by an incorrect implementation of the XOR function in our algorithm. Specifically, the line `if ((a ^ b) == 0)` should be replaced with `if (a == b)` to ensure accurate results.

I have updated the code accordingly and re-run the tests, which have all passed successfully. I'm confident that this resolves the issue and I apologize again for any inconvenience it may have caused.
