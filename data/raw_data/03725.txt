I've been reviewing our supplier relations and I'm not satisfied with the current state. The revised specs for the forthcoming batch of components should address these concerns. Specifically, I've added stricter quality control measures and clearer communication channels to prevent misunderstandings. However, I'm still grappling with the implications of this overhaul on our existing relationships. I'd appreciate your input on how we can navigate this transition while maintaining a positive working environment.
