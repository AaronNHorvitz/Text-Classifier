Dear Donna,

As I navigate the labyrinthine corridors of Cardiff Electric's Engineering department, I find myself pondering the notion of "volunteer opportunities". It seems an oxymoron - as if we're somehow obligated to donate our time and expertise to a company that already drains us dry. Nevertheless, I shall play along.

I've noticed that some colleagues have taken it upon themselves to spearhead various projects outside of their regular duties. While I applaud the initiative, I must admit that my own enthusiasm is waning. The isolation of working on these solo endeavors only serves to amplify the feeling of disconnection from the rest of the team.

If you're considering taking on a volunteer project, I'd caution against it. It's a fleeting high, followed by an existential crisis. Trust me, I know.
