Dear Colleagues,

I am pleased to announce the launch of our Green Office Initiative, a comprehensive plan aimed at reducing our environmental footprint. This initiative encompasses several key areas including waste reduction, energy saving, and sustainable commuting options.

**Initiative Highlights:**
- Installation of energy-efficient lighting throughout the office
- Implementation of recycling stations at every floor
- Incentives for employees using public transportation or carpooling

We encourage everyone to participate actively and contribute ideas on how we can further enhance our environmental efforts. Please join us for a kickoff meeting on Monday, November 17, 2025, at 1:00 PM in the main auditorium where we will discuss the program in detail and answer any questions you may have.

Together, we can make a significant impact on our environment.
