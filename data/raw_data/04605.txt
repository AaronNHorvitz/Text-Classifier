Subject:    Industry News - IBM's PC/AT Architecture


Dear Gordon, Debbie, and Ed,

I'm delighted to share some interesting news from the industry. IBM has just announced their new PC/AT architecture, which promises improved performance and compatibility with existing software. I've taken a look at the specs and noticed that they're utilizing a 6.5 MHz clock speed, which is a significant increase over the original PC's 4.77 MHz.

The new architecture also includes an 80286 processor, which should provide better multitasking capabilities. I've included some key points from IBM's press release below:

"The PC/AT architecture is designed to support high-performance applications and multi-user environments."
"It features a 16-bit address bus, allowing for up to 64KB of RAM."

I think this development has significant implications for our own projects at Cardiff Electric. I'd love to discuss the potential applications and implications further.
