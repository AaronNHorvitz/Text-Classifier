Dear Yo-Yo,

I'm writing to provide an update on our product recall efforts. As you know, we've been experiencing issues with the GORDON-1's power supply module. After conducting a thorough analysis, I've identified several key factors contributing to the problem.

Firstly, the PCB design is flawed, allowing excessive voltage fluctuations to occur. Secondly, the component selection is subpar, leading to premature failure of critical components. Lastly, our testing procedures were inadequate, resulting in missed defects.

I propose we implement a revised manufacturing process, incorporating new PCB designs and upgraded components. Additionally, I recommend increasing our testing regime to include more rigorous stress-testing and failure analysis.

Please let me know if you'd like to discuss these findings further or schedule a meeting to review the details.
