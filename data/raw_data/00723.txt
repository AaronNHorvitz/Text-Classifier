I'm writing to express my disappointment with the current state of our quality assurance process. As Chief Engineer, I've been tasked with optimizing the production line, but the QA procedures we're using are hindering progress.

Our testing protocols are too broad and don't account for the nuances of our proprietary hardware. This means we're wasting valuable time and resources on unnecessary rework. I'd like to discuss this further and explore ways to integrate these changes into our existing workflow.
