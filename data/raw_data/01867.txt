Dear Ed, Joe, Barry, Donna, and Larry,

I am compelled to address the disparity between projected and actual expenses within the Engineering department. As Chief Engineer, I have taken it upon myself to scrutinize the current financial situation, and I fear we are on a precipice of insolvency if we do not take immediate action.

In reviewing our budget, I have identified several areas where costs can be optimized without compromising our R&D efforts. Specifically, I propose streamlining procurement procedures and renegotiating contracts with our primary suppliers. Additionally, I suggest implementing a more stringent approval process for non-essential expenses to prevent unnecessary financial hemorrhaging.

I request that each of you submit your updated expense reports to me by the end of this quarter, as we must have an accurate accounting of our expenditures in order to make informed decisions moving forward.
