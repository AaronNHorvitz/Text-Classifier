Subject: Compliance Issue with Mainframe Access


Hi Yo-Yo, Malcolm, Gordon, John, and Joe,

I'm reaching out to provide some guidance on a compliance issue we've been experiencing with mainframe access. As you know, our team has been working diligently to ensure our systems are up to date and secure. Recently, we've encountered an issue where certain users are unable to access specific files and folders within the mainframe.

After conducting a thorough investigation, I've identified that this issue is caused by a misconfigured permissions setting. To resolve this, please follow these steps:

1. Log in to the mainframe using your authorized credentials.
2. Navigate to the affected folder and right-click on it.
3. Select "Properties" and click on the "Security" tab.
4. Ensure that the correct user groups are listed with read-write permissions.

If you're still experiencing issues after completing these steps, please don't hesitate to reach out to me directly. I'll be happy to provide further assistance.
