Congratulations. Yeah, congratulations are in order. You're always quick to pat us on the back when we're doing something right, and that's great. So yeah, congratulations to everyone involved. Enjoy it while it lasts. Because in our world, that's exactly what it is: a fleeting moment of glory before the next challenge comes along.
