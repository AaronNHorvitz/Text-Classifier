Dear Joe, Barry, Donna, and Ed,

As we continue to develop our training programs, I wanted to bring up some concerns regarding structured learning. In my experience, formalized instruction can be beneficial for knowledge transfer, but I'm worried that our current approach may not be taking full advantage of this concept.

For instance, have you considered implementing a more modularized curriculum? This would allow us to build upon existing knowledge and create a more cohesive learning framework. I've attached a simple example of how we could structure this using XML:

<curriculum>
  <module id="1">
    <topic>Introduction to Structured Learning</topic>
    <learning_objectives>
      <objective>Define structured learning principles</objective>
      <objective>Explain the benefits of modularized curriculum</objective>
    </learning_objectives>
  </module>
  <!-- Continue with additional modules -->
</curriculum>

I believe this approach could greatly enhance our training effectiveness. I'd appreciate any feedback or suggestions you may have on this topic.
