Hi everyone,

I'm thrilled to share some exciting volunteer opportunities that have recently become available within Cardiff Electric. As a member of the Technical Support team, I've had the pleasure of getting involved with these initiatives and can attest to the positive impact they've had on our company culture.

One notable opportunity is our ongoing partnership with the local coding club for underprivileged youth. We're always looking for enthusiastic volunteers to help mentor and teach these young minds the ins and outs of computer programming. Not only do we get to give back to the community, but it's also a fantastic way to learn new skills and build relationships with like-minded individuals.

Another great opportunity is our collaboration with the University of Texas at Austin's Computer Science department. We're seeking volunteers to help judge their annual hackathon competition, where students showcase their innovative projects and ideas. It's an incredible way to see the next generation of tech innovators in action and provide valuable feedback to help them grow.

If you're interested in learning more or getting involved, please don't hesitate to reach out. I'd be more than happy to answer any questions or set up a meeting with our volunteer coordinators.
