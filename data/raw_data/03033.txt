Hi Ed and Joe,

I've been pouring over the latest system architecture designs, and I'm convinced that we're on the cusp of a breakthrough. The new CPU architecture is going to revolutionize our approach to data processing, but it requires some critical changes to the existing codebase. I've outlined the necessary revisions below:

1. Redundant buffer arrays must be updated to accommodate the new instruction set.
2. The memory management unit needs to be reworked to account for the increased cache sizes.
3. A secondary validation step is required to prevent data corruption during file transfers.

These changes are non-negotiable; they're the only way to ensure our system remains stable and efficient moving forward. I'd appreciate your prompt attention to this matter, as it will have a significant impact on our overall project timeline.
