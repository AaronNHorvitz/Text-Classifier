Dear Larry,

EMAIL START's precision and attention to detail have always been my greatest strengths. And yet, here I am, writing an email that reeks of jealousy. It seems Cardiff Electric has finally decided to greenlight the new project, and I'm not exactly thrilled about it.

Let's just say I had hoped for a more... substantial role in this endeavor. Instead, I find myself relegated to mere observer status. The thought of those young whippersnappers at Bream getting all the glory is enough to make my blood boil.

I suppose it's only natural that the company would want to tap into their youthful energy and innovative spirit. But can't they see that I'm the one with the real expertise here? Ah, but I digress.

In any case, I wanted to express my gratitude for your help in getting this project off the ground. Your enthusiasm is infectious, and I must admit it's been a pleasure working with you thus far.
