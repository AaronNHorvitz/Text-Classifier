Dear Larry,

I've been pouring over the latest supplier proposals, and I'm compelled to express my concerns regarding the high-speed interface specifications. It's imperative that we establish a clear set of guidelines for component integration, lest we invite chaos into our systems.

The current proposals from Intel and Texas Instruments are woefully inadequate. Their reluctance to adopt the new 8-bit protocol is not only shortsighted but also jeopardizes the entire project. I implore you to reconsider our options and explore more innovative solutions.

In my opinion, the answer lies in reworking the existing architecture to accommodate the new protocol. It's a risk, I admit, but one that could yield exponential gains in efficiency and reliability. I'd be happy to discuss this further with you.
