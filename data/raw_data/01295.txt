I wanted to follow up on our previous discussions regarding the recent system outage. As we've determined, the issue was caused by an unexpected interaction between the database queries and the caching mechanism.

To prevent future occurrences, I recommend implementing a timeout parameter for the database connections, as outlined in the following snippet:

```
db.connect(timeout=10)
```

This will allow our application to detect and handle these types of issues more effectively.
