Dear All,

I'm writing to express my frustration and disappointment regarding the lack of technical expertise among our volunteers. As Chief Engineer, I expect a certain level of proficiency from those who claim to be willing to assist us. Unfortunately, my experience thus far has been underwhelming.

In particular, I've noticed that Larry Goins' attempts at coding have resulted in more bugs than beneficial changes. Donna Clark's enthusiasm is admirable, but her lack of understanding of our systems only leads to confusion and wasted time. Malcolm Levitan's claims of expertise are laughable, given his inability to grasp even the most basic concepts. Barry Shields'... well, let's just say I've lost count of how many times he's "accidentally" deleted crucial data.

I implore you all: if you're going to volunteer your time and resources, please have some basic understanding of what we do here at Cardiff Electric. This is not a social club or a playground for the tech-illiterate. We are an engineering department, and our work requires precision and attention to detail.
