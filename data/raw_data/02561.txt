I'm writing to provide a preliminary analysis of the system outage that occurred yesterday. As Chief Engineer, I've been reviewing the logs and conducting tests to identify the root cause.

Preliminary findings suggest that the issue is related to a faulty power supply unit (PSU) in the mainframe. Further testing will be required to confirm this hypothesis, but I believe it's essential to rule out this possibility before exploring other causes.

I've identified several variables that could have contributed to the PSU failure: excessive voltage fluctuations during the power outage, inadequate cooling, and possibly, a manufacturing defect. I'll need to run some additional simulations to determine which factor is most likely responsible.

In light of these findings, I recommend we prioritize the PSU replacement as our top priority. This will ensure the system's reliability in the short term while we continue to investigate other potential causes.
