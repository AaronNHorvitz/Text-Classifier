I'm thrilled to report that our team was able to isolate and resolve the 80386 processor issue we've been experiencing. Your prompt feedback enabled us to troubleshoot the problem efficiently, and I'm confident this fix will prevent future occurrences.

The root cause of the issue was a software conflict with an outdated BIOS version. We updated the BIOS to the latest revision, which resolved the problem. To confirm the fix, please follow these steps:

1. Boot up your system and enter the BIOS setup.
2. Verify that the BIOS version has been updated to 1.23 or later.
3. Save changes and exit the BIOS setup.

This should ensure your processor is functioning correctly moving forward.
