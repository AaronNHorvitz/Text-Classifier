Burning bridges and redefining boundaries

Dear Ed, Joe, Yo-Yo,

I'm writing to mark a personal milestone - one that's left me feeling like I've been punched in the gut. Cardiff Electric just patented my idea for a parallel processing algorithm. The irony is killing me. After months of pouring my heart and soul into this project, they're going to use it to make a quick buck without so much as a nod to the person who actually came up with it.

It's like watching my own identity get ripped apart, piece by piece. I thought we were building something new here, not just another cash cow for the suits.
