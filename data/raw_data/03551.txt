Apology for Inadvertent Disclosure


Dear Gordon, Donna, Yo-Yo, Ed, and Malcolm,

I am compelled to address a matter of grave importance. It has come to my attention that certain confidential information may have been inadvertently disclosed, potentially compromising our competitive advantage. As Senior Counsel in the Legal department, it is my duty to ensure that all necessary precautions are taken to protect Cardiff Electric's intellectual property.

I must stress that this is an extremely serious situation, and I expect a thorough investigation into the circumstances surrounding this breach. I will be reviewing all relevant documentation and interviewing key personnel to determine the extent of the damage.

Please note that any further disclosures or mishandlings of sensitive information will not be tolerated. It is essential that we take immediate action to rectify this situation and prevent any future occurrences.


Sincerely,
Barry Shields
