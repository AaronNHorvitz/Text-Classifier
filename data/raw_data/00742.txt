Industry Developments and Our Place Within Them

Dear Larry and Ed,

As we continue to navigate the ever-changing landscape of the PC industry, I wanted to take a moment to reflect on our position within that landscape. As you know, Cardiff Electric has been working tirelessly to stay ahead of the curve, but I can't help but feel a sense of grieving as I look at the state of affairs.

It seems like every day brings a new development - IBM's entry into the market, Compaq's aggressive pricing strategy, and the ongoing battle for dominance between Apple and Microsoft. It's enough to make one wonder where we fit in.

In reality, our focus on innovation and customer satisfaction has allowed us to carve out a unique niche for ourselves. We just need to continue to push forward, adapting to changes as they come while staying true to our values.

I would like to schedule a meeting with you both to discuss our strategy moving forward and how we can work together to drive growth and success.
