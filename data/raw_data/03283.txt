Dear Barry, Cameron, Ed,

I am compelled to address several health and safety concerns that have recently arisen in our department. As Chief Engineer, I take my responsibility for ensuring a safe working environment very seriously. However, I must admit that certain... irregularities have caught my attention.

Specifically, I have observed instances of equipment being operated without proper clearance, and personnel not adhering to established protocols for handling hazardous materials. I am concerned that these lapses may ultimately compromise our ability to maintain the highest standards of quality and performance in our products.

I would appreciate it if each of you could take a moment to review our department's safety procedures and report any concerns or suggestions you may have. I believe that by working together, we can address these issues proactively and ensure a safe and healthy work environment for everyone.

Sincerely,
