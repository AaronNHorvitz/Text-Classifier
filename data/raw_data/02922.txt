I wanted to follow up on our previous discussions regarding connectivity issues with some of our Series 1 systems. After conducting a thorough analysis, I've identified a few key areas that may be contributing to the problem. 

Firstly, please ensure that all cables are securely connected and free from damage or corrosion. Secondly, verify that the system's serial number is correctly entered in the configuration menu. If either of these checks out, it's possible that there's an issue with the network settings. In this case, try resetting the network adapter to its default settings.

If none of these steps resolve the issue, please don't hesitate to reach out and I'll be happy to assist you further.
