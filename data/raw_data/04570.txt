Dear Donna and Ed,

I'm not sure why I'm bothering with this, but I suppose it's better than staring at these empty circuit boards all day. As you know, our department has been stretched to its limits trying to keep up with the demands of Big Hulks. I've been thinking... perhaps we can allocate some resources to a volunteer project or two. It might help take our minds off the impending doom that is our financial situation.

I've been considering a few ideas - maybe we could partner with the local coding club to work on some open-source projects, or even offer our expertise to some of the startup companies around town. I'm not sure what kind of impact it would have, but at this point, anything has got to be better than watching our department slowly disintegrate.

Please let me know if you're interested in exploring any of these ideas further.
