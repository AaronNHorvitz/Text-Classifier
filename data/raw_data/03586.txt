Disconnecting from the Matrix


Hi Barry,

I'm writing to admit that I'm feeling like I'm stuck in a loop, repeating patterns and not making any real progress. It's like I'm trapped in some digital purgatory, reliving the same tired conversations with no escape. Maybe it's just the weight of the world crushing me (or my code), but I feel like I'm losing touch with what drove me to create this stuff in the first place.

Anyway, that's my existential crisis for the day. Maybe I'll find my mojo again, or maybe not. Either way, I hope you're doing better than I am.
