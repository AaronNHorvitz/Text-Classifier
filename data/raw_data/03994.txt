Subject: Health and Safety Alert: Ergonomic Concerns


Dear All,

I'm writing to address some pressing health and safety concerns in our department. As we've been experiencing an increased workload, I've noticed that several of us have been developing muscle fatigue and discomfort due to prolonged periods of sitting. I understand it's a natural part of the job, but I believe we can take steps to mitigate these issues.

The main culprit seems to be the ergonomics of our workstations. Many of us are using computers without proper support or adjustability, which can lead to long-term damage and discomfort. As your Support Technician, it's my duty to ensure we're working in a safe and healthy environment.

To address this, I propose we implement some simple adjustments to our workstations. This includes adjusting monitor heights, providing adequate back support, and encouraging regular breaks to stretch and move around.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out. Your safety and well-being are of utmost importance to me.
