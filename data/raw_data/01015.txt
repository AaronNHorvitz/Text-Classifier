Dear Ed, Donna, and Joe,

I am compelled to notify you of an internal matter that requires my attention. Despite my best efforts to maintain a neutral demeanor, I find myself struggling to muster the requisite enthusiasm for this announcement.

In compliance with company policies, Cardiff Electric has initiated a thorough review of our current product line. As part of this process, we will be discontinuing certain underperforming lines and refocusing on more profitable ventures.

I understand that this news may cause uncertainty among some colleagues; however, I am confident that this decision will ultimately benefit the company in the long run.

Please direct any inquiries or concerns to my office.
