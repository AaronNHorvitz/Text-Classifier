Dear John, Donna, and Malcolm,

I'm writing to report a system outage that occurred at 14:45 hours today. The incident was triggered by an unexpected reboot of our primary server, which subsequently cascaded through the network. Our monitoring systems failed to detect the anomaly in time to initiate corrective action.

The root cause analysis indicates a possible hardware failure or misconfiguration on the server's power supply unit (PSU). I've scheduled a visual inspection and testing for tomorrow morning to confirm this hypothesis.

I'll update you all as more information becomes available.
