Hi Barry,

I'm surprised you'd even suggest that volunteering is a waste of company resources. I think you're misunderstanding the value of giving back to the community. When we give, we receive in return - and I don't just mean karma. I mean genuine connections with people, new perspectives, and a sense of purpose.

You may be Senior Counsel, but I'm Vice President of Sales, and I know that building relationships and fostering loyalty are essential to our success. And what better way to do that than by giving back?

I understand your concerns about protocol and guidelines, but sometimes the best innovations come from thinking outside the box (or in this case, the cubicle). I urge you to consider the long-term benefits of investing in our community - it's not just a feel-good exercise; it's good business.

I urge you all to prioritize your professional obligations and refrain from exploring volunteer opportunities during working hours. Any deviation from this expectation will be met with swift and severe repercussions.
