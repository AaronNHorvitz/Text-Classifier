Subject: Product Recall: Analysis and Recommendations


Hello,

I've been working late nights to get a handle on the product recall situation. The preliminary findings indicate that the faulty component was a custom-made PCB from our supplier, ElectroPulse. I've reviewed their manufacturing processes and it appears they used an outdated design that doesn't meet our specifications.

The affected products are limited to 500 units of the PC-Clone, which we shipped last quarter. I recommend recalling these units immediately to prevent any further issues or potential harm to customers. In my opinion, this is a high-priority issue that requires immediate attention.

Please review the attached analysis for more details on the faulty component and recommended corrective actions.
