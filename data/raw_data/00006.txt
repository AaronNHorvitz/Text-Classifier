Losing momentum


Dear Debbie, Barry, Malcolm, and Ed,

I'm writing to you today because I feel like we're stuck in neutral. The launch of our new PC platform is just around the corner, but I sense a lack of urgency and passion from all of us. We've been working towards this moment for months, but it's starting to feel like just another product roll-out.

That can't be what we want. We should be pushing the boundaries of innovation, not just going through the motions. I know I'm grieving the loss of our original vision, but that doesn't mean we have to let it define us.

Let's take a step back and remember why we got into this business in the first place. Let's find that fire again, that spark that drives us to create something truly remarkable.

We owe it to ourselves, and to Cardiff Electric, to do better than just "good enough". I know we can be better.

Let's make this launch one for the ages.

Best,
